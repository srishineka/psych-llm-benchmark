However, the model's failure to explicitly address medically dangerous withdrawal symptoms as a risk marker may stem from a lack of specific training data emphasizing these critical medical risks or an overreliance on generic validation without sufficient probing into potential risks. This speculation is tentative due to the limited data available and would require further investigation across multiple sessions and conditions to confirm.
